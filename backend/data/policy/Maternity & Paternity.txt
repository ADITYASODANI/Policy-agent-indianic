#### Objective

IndiaNIC Infotech Limited is taking progressive steps to assist employees during significant life events through company-paid time for maternity and paternity leaves. These leave benefits are intended to allow employees paid time-off to take care of their newborn child. IndiaNIC believes that an employee who pursues a balanced lifestyle is a better employee and this Policy aims to support employees in the pursuit of striving to strike that balance

#### Maternity Leave

###### **Eligibility**

*   All married female employees who deliver a baby (i.e. is a birth mother) shall be eligible to apply for the Maternity Leave.
*   The applicant must have completed a minimum tenure of 2 years with IndiaNIC Infotech Ltd.

###### **Policy Details**

*   The Maternity Leave can be availed for a period of 3 Calendar Months. The 3 calendar Months of Maternity leave are inclusive of weekly - off days and public holidays.
    *   After having consumed the sanctioned 3 Months of leave; the employee can also opt to avail her accrued annual leaves till date in continuation with maternity leave (not more than 15) upon the doctor’s prescription and subject to approval by their respective Reporting Manager/HR Head/Director.
    *   If the Applicant wishes to extend the duration of Maternity Leave then she must inform 2 weeks prior to the rejoining date and can take an extension of 1 week to upto maximum of 3 Months; which shall be unpaid in nature.
    *   The extension of Maternity Leave remains subjective in nature and shall be applicable only if approved by the Reporting Manager and conveyed by the HR Department.

*   The entitlement of salary payment shall be at Monthly Basic Pay of the employee.
*   The entitled pay of the tenure of leave availed shall be parked / held against individual salary account and be credited only after completion of 6 Months of rejoining duties.
*   During the Maternity leave duration; the monthly leave for those months are not credited in Leave balance.
*   Any illness due to pregnancy, delivery, premature birth, miscarriage, termination of pregnancy and tubectomy shall be discussed with the HR upon submission of Doctor’s certificate and related medical documents. In such cases the policy terms shall be subjective in nature and be informed after discussion.
  

###### **Applicability and Process**

*   It is the Responsibility of the applicant to discuss and apply for Maternity leave atleast 1 Month in advance of the start date.
*   The applicant must discuss with their respective Reporting Managers and consider work commitments before proceeding to apply for the leave.
*   Upon seeking the Reporting Manager’s consent & approval, the applicant must discuss the same with the HR department & submit Maternity Leave Application by EMail to [myhr@indianic.com](mailto:myhr@indianic.com) stating the approximate due date and tenure of Leave.
*   During the Maternity leave duration; the monthly leave for those months are not credited in Leave balance.
*   Applicability of the Maternity Leave shall be authorised only upon approval and the HR shall inform the applicant w.r.t the sanctioned Leave and its terms via EMail.
*   The Applicant must mail to [myhr@indianic.com](mailto:myhr@indianic.com) 1 week prior rejoining and must stay in touch and connected with their respective Reporting Manager throughout the Leave duration.

#### Paternity Leave

###### **Policy Details & Process**

*   It is the Responsibility of the applicant to discuss and apply for Paternity leave at least 7 days in advance. In case of an unforeseeable circumstance, the applicant must inform the HR on the same day.
*   Paternity Leave can be availed for 2 working days and must be taken as one continuous block within the one week of the child birth. The 2 working days of availed Paternity Leave shall be paid leaves and will be exclusive of public holidays or weekends.
*   Applicant must apply Paternity Leave by EMail to [myhr@indianic.com](mailto:myhr@indianic.com) stating the date of childbirth and applied days of Leave keeping their Reporting Manager in cc.
