IndiaNIC INFOTECH LIMITED
CODE OF CONDUCT & ETHICS
Effective Date: 01 January 2026
Policy No. & Version: HR / 2026 / Code of Conduct & Ethics / Version 2
Issuing Department: Human Resources
Applicability: All locations, including Office, Client Sites, and Remote Work






1. PREAMBLE
IndiaNIC Infotech Limited (“IndiaNIC” or “the Company”) is committed to maintaining the highest standards of professional conduct, integrity, transparency, and ethical behavior in all its operations.

This Code of Conduct & Ethics (“Code”) establishes the principles and expectations governing behavior at the workplace and in all professional interactions. The Code aims to promote a safe, respectful, inclusive, and legally compliant work environment, while safeguarding the interests of employees, clients, partners, and the Company.

This Code forms an integral part of the terms and conditions of employment and applies equally to all employees, directors, officers, consultants, trainees, interns, and contractual staff.

2. APPLICABILITY & LEGAL FRAMEWORK
This Code is framed in accordance with applicable Indian laws, including but not limited to:

Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946


Industrial Disputes Act, 1947


Gujarat Shops and Establishments Act


Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition & Redressal) Act, 2013


Information Technology Act, 2000


Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023


Applicable rules, notifications, and amendments


This Code applies to employees working from:

Company offices

Client locations

Remote / Work-From-Home (WFH) arrangements

Contractual and consultants also be liable for above conducts


3. EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITY & COMPLIANCE
All covered individuals are expected to:

Read, understand, and comply with this Code


Act in the best interests of the Company and its stakeholders


Seek clarification from their Reporting Manager or HR in case of doubt


Violation of this Code may result in disciplinary action, including termination of employment and/or legal action, depending on the severity of the breach.

4. EMPLOYEE HEALTH, SAFETY & WORKPLACE SECURITY
IndiaNIC is committed to providing a safe, secure, and healthy work environment.

Employees must:

Follow all safety, security, and emergency procedures


Wear Company-issued ID cards within office premises


Report safety hazards, accidents, or suspicious activities immediately


The following are strictly prohibited on Company premises:

Smoking, alcohol, tobacco, or substance abuse:

Any employee found carrying, consuming, storing, distributing, or being under the influence of the above substances will be considered in breach of the Company Code of Conduct and shall be subject to immediate disciplinary action, which may include warning, suspension, or termination, depending on the severity of the violation.


Unauthorized access to restricted areas, systems, or data


Employees are responsible for safeguarding their personal belongings. The Company shall not be liable for personal loss or damage.



5. CONFLICT OF INTEREST, WORKPLACE DECORUM, PROFESSIONAL BEHAVIOR, DISCIPLINE

Conflict of Interest
Employees, especially those in managerial or decision-making roles, must avoid situations where personal interests conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of the Company.

Any direct or indirect association, partnership, advisory role, or financial interest in another business that may compete with or influence Company decisions must be disclosed in advance and approved as per Company policy.

Failure to disclose such conflicts may result in appropriate action as per Company guidelines.

Employees are expected to maintain professionalism, discipline, and respectful conduct at all times.

Employees must:

Communicate courteously and professionally


Maintain cleanliness and orderly behavior


Adhere to attendance, access control, and workplace guidelines


Use Company resources responsibly



Any conduct that disrupts workplace harmony or damages the Company’s reputation will be treated as misconduct.



6. PREVENTION OF SEXUAL HARASSMENT (POSH)
IndiaNIC is committed to providing a workplace free from sexual harassment.

Sexual harassment in any form—physical, verbal, visual, or psychological—is strictly prohibited.


The Company has constituted an Internal Committee (IC) in accordance with the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2013.


Complaints may be raised through officially designated channels.


Confidentiality, fair inquiry, and protection against retaliation are assured.


Violations will attract strict disciplinary action and may result in legal proceedings.

7. EQUAL OPPORTUNITY & NON-DISCRIMINATION
IndiaNIC is an equal opportunity employer.

Employment decisions shall be based solely on merit, performance, and business requirements.

8. DISCIPLINE & MISCONDUCT
Employees must comply with all Company policies, lawful instructions, and contractual obligations.

Misconduct includes, but is not limited to:

Harassment, abuse, or discriminatory behavior


Negligence, insubordination, or refusal to perform assigned duties


Unauthorized absence or habitual late coming


Data theft, system misuse, or breach of IT security


Falsification of records or expense claims


Substance abuse or violence


Damage to Company or client property

Disciplinary action shall follow principles of natural justice, including fair inquiry and opportunity to be heard.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY, DATA PROTECTION & INFORMATION SECURITY
All Company, client, and third-party information is strictly confidential.

Employees must:

Protect confidential information during and after employment

Points discussed during team meetings and stated as "confidential" for relevant departments should not be shared with other cross departments, employees.

Not disclose information without authorization or legal obligation

Follow cybersecurity, information security, and data protection policies

Respect intellectual property rights


Personal data shall be processed in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Unauthorized disclosure, including via social media or AI tools, will be treated as a serious breach.

10. REMOTE WORK & WORK-FROM-HOME (WFH) RESPONSIBILITIES
Employees working remotely must ensure:

Secure internet connections and protected devices


No unauthorized access to Company or client data


Compliance with defined working hours and availability expectations


Adherence to client confidentiality and data security requirements


11. USE & PROTECTION OF COMPANY ASSETS
Company assets—including IT systems, laptops, software, networks, licenses, and data—must be used strictly for official purposes.

Use of pirated or unlicensed software is prohibited


Assets must be returned upon separation


Internet and system misuse is prohibited


12. ANTI-BRIBERY & ANTI-CORRUPTION
IndiaNIC follows a zero-tolerance policy towards bribery and corruption.

Employees shall not offer, solicit, accept, or facilitate bribes, kickbacks, or improper advantages in any form.

13. WHISTLEBLOWER & ETHICAL REPORTING
Employees may report unethical conduct, policy violations, harassment, or legal non-compliance without fear of retaliation. (platform to report anonymously : https://nazar.indianic.biz/ 

Reports may be made confidentially to HR or designated authorities. Retaliation against whistleblowers will result in disciplinary action.

14. INTERPRETATION & AMENMENT
This Code shall be interpreted in accordance with applicable laws.
 The Company reserves the right to amend this Code from time to time.
 Interpretation shall rest with the Head – Human Resources.
